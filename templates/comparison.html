Placement Success Rate
Takeaway: The Importance of Reliability

The Hybrid-AI achieves an outstanding placement success rate of over 25%, a direct result of its intelligent fallback system. In contrast, all other algorithms, including the baseline `AI-Predictor`, have lower success rates, making them less suitable for production environments where reliability is paramount.

SLA Violation Analysis
Takeaway: Stability as a Feature

The baseline `AI-Predictor` was one of the worst offenders for SLA violations because it packed hosts too aggressively. The Hybrid-AI, by contrast, has the lowest number of violations by a significant margin. Its multi-objective training taught it to prioritize stability alongside efficiency.

Energy Consumption Analysis
Takeaway: Intelligent Efficiency

The Hybrid-AI achieves a significant reduction in energy consumption compared to other algorithms. It does this not by simply spreading load like `Worst-Fit`, but by intelligently consolidating VMs onto the most energy-efficient hosts while avoiding the high-utilization power penalties.

Cost Analysis
Takeaway: Financial Prudence

Similarly, the Hybrid-AI significantly cuts operational costs. The cost model penalizes high utilization, a factor the baseline `AI-Predictor` ignored. The `Hybrid-AI` understands this trade-off and optimizes for the lowest possible cost without sacrificing performance, demonstrating true financial intelligence.

CPU Utilization Analysis
Takeaway: Effective CPU Management

The Hybrid-AI maintains an impressive CPU utilization of over 95%. It avoids the dangerously high utilization of the baseline AI (which led to SLA violations) and the inefficiently low utilization of `Worst-Fit`, finding the optimal balance for CPU resources.

RAM Utilization Analysis
Takeaway: Balanced Memory Allocation

Just as with CPU, the Hybrid-AI achieves a strong RAM utilization of over 77%. This demonstrates its ability to avoid over-packing a single resource type. It understands that true data center efficiency comes from using all resources in harmony, not maximizing one at the expense of another.

Final Verdict

The evidence is conclusive. While simple algorithms can appear effective when viewed through a single lens, they are brittle and inefficient in a complex, multi-objective environment. The baseline `AI-Predictor` failed because it was a single-minded tool for a multi-faceted problem.

The Hybrid-AI model has proven itself to be the only solution that delivers on all fronts: high reliability, significant cost and energy savings, and high, balanced efficiency. It is the definitive and superior choice for modern data center resource management.
